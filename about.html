Class 10 School Project

Why I Chose Cinema for My School Project

As a Class 10 student preparing for board exams, my days are filled with solving quadratic equations, balancing chemical equations, and memorizing physics laws. But outside the classroom, I’ve always turned to movies to understand how those ideas live in the real world. Cinema proved to me that science isn't just dry lines on a chalkboard—it is Homer Hickam studying trigonometry in October Sky to build rockets, William Kamkwamba turning scrap metal into a wind turbine in Malawi, and Katherine Johnson calculating orbital trajectories at NASA.

For my annual school project, instead of making a typical slide deck or poster board, I wanted to create something lasting: an interactive digital monograph exploring 14 films that shaped how I think about science, ethics, courage, and my future. Each movie here represents a life lesson that textbook chapters alone couldn't teach me—from the neuroscience of emotions in Inside Out to the quiet resilience needed to overcome academic self-doubt in Akeelah and the Bee.

I built this website using pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, applying what I learned in computer science with my love for stories. I hope my classmates, teachers, and visitors find something here that inspires them to dream bigger.

Milestones

How My School Project Developed

PHASE 1 — CURATING THE 14 FILMS

Connecting Cinema to High School Science & Ethics

Selecting 14 defining films that bridge our high school curriculum—physics, robotics, mathematics, psychology, and environmental science—with human perseverance.

PHASE 2 — RESEARCH & ESSAY WRITING

Analyzing Themes from a Student Perspective

Drafting in-depth synopses, personal reviews, favorite scenes, and key life takeaways for each title as an academic study.

PHASE 3 — THE DIGITAL WEB MONOGRAPH

A Pure Vanilla Code Presentation

Coded entirely from scratch in vanilla HTML5, CSS custom properties, canvas 2D falling Sakura physics, ambient sound, and Web Speech narration for accessibility.

Computer Science Practical

The Code & Technology Behind My Project

Built without complex framework dependencies or build tools. As part of my computer science learning, I relied on pure semantic HTML5, modern CSS custom properties, and modular vanilla JavaScript.

VANILLA ES MODULES

Zero bundlers, zero framework overhead. Clean modular JavaScript architecture.

GSAP & SCROLLTRIGGER

Silky editorial motion, text mask reveals, and GSAP Flip grid animations.

LENIS SMOOTH SCROLL

Physics-based momentum scrolling synced seamlessly with ScrollTrigger tickers.

WEB SPEECH SYNTHESIS

Sentence-chunked audio narration with real-time text highlight tracking.

CANVAS 2D SAKURA PHYSICS

DPR-aware falling petal engine with mouse repulsion and scroll wind gusts.

WEB AUDIO SYNTHESIZER

Generative pentatonic ambient chords and subtle UI feedback without external dependencies.
